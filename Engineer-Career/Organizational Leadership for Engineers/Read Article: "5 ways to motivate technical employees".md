# Read Article: "5 ways to motivate technical employees"

Ref: https://opensource.com/article/17/5/5-ways-to-motivate-technical-employees

1. Understand the difference between a leader and a manager

Great leaders understand that their role is to get the best out of individuals to achieve a shared goal.

2. Provide a clear vision

You can't motivate technical employees on sound only; conveying a clear vision of your goal is vital to get the attention and buy-in of those you are trying to lead and motivate.

3. Provide direction, then let people do what they do

Micromanaging tasks is most effective when it is self-managed by the technical employee.

4. Let smart people be smart

"Tell me what you want done or tell me how to do it." You can't have both if you are trying to motivate the smart individuals you are organizationally responsible for getting to execute the vision.

5. Positive, public feedback has much greater value than a bonus

A public acknowledgment is often a very low-cost/high-value way of positively motivating your employees.
