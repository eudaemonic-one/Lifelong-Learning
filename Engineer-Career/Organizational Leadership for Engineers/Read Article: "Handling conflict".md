# Read Article: "Handling conflict"

The goal of conflict is getting to performing and growing the team. Conflict is normal.

## Yes to the person and no to the conflict

**Finding a common viewpoint!**

How to resolve conflicts?
* define causes
* understanding points of view
* clarifying preferences
* examining alternatives
* gaining commitments from these involved that they are prepared to work towards finding solutions

Conflict can be resolved at 3 different levels:
* Mutual recognition of a known rule or policy or principle and agreement on its application.
* In the case of the principle you must ask a closed question to get a yes response.
* Mutual agreement before things get out of control.

## The mechanics of conflict

* Thoughtlessness
* Frustration
* Conflict occurs when thoughtlessness meets frustration.

## Stopping it from starting

Prevention is better than cure.

Avoid conflict by keeping a sense of proportion: identify and deal with those little irritants before they grow into major problems.
