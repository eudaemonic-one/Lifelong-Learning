# Read Article: "Breaking down inter-team & departmental barriers"

## Internal customers: who they are and their rights and responsibilities

Silo mentality in organizations is prevalent.

A team is not only reliant on the accountability of its own members, butr also on other teams or departments.

## The rights and responsibilities of colleagues

Rights:
* respect
* information to tasks
* resources necessary for the completion of their tasks
* freedom and opportunity to express views and opinions
* understanding
* support
* honest, ethical, moral and legal conduct

Responsibilities:
* being available, approachable and responsive
* listening fairly and uncritically to others' views and opinions
* sharing decision making when this affects others' work
* providing requested information and resources
* being aware of others' personal beliefs and needs

Internal relationships:
* Identify your internal customers and suppliers
* Understand your customers
* Measure where you are
* Set quality service standards
* Involve your team
* Monitor regularly
* Solve problems

## Influencing your internal customers

Through the creation of a cross-functional team.

Be rationale in your explanation of ideas and present benefits to senior management.

Interpersonal skills:
* strategic orientation and the ability to exercise strategic control
* a strong sense of practicality
* stimulation of action by creating constructive dissatisfaction
* strong intuition, sensitivity and willingness to experiment
* broad influence in the organization and its surroudings
* willingness to listen, explain and offer assitance
* willingness to take risks, decisions and responsibility
* strong performance drive and exemplary behavior that motivates and inspires employee
* guidance of employees rather than issuing orders
* authority through skill rather than hierarchical position
* use of events as a means of demonstrating and promting policy

Have a sponsor at a more senior level to support your activities.

Build a web of influence so that people can all make a contribution to your working activities, by sharing knowledge, informal advice, political information about who are the real power brokers.

Good influencing is all about inspiring peopel to act according to their own motivations.

As always client centred, is their experience of you and your team and how that experience can be enhanced and another point is probe to discover new ways in which you can improve their performance and reinforce the warm feelings that their initial purchase gave them.
