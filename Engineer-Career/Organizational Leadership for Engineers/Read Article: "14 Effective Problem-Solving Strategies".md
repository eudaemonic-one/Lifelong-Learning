# Read Article: "14 Effective Problem-Solving Strategies"

* Define the problem
* Visualize the problem
* Draw a diagram of the problem
* Break the problem into smaller pieces
* Redefine the problem
* Collect and organize information about the problem
* Work backward
* Use the Kipling Method
  * What is the problem?
  * Why is the problem important?
  * When did the problem arise and when does it need to be solved?
  * How did the problem happen?
  * Where is the problem occuring?
  * Who does the problem affect?
* Use your past experience
* Bring in a facilitator
* Consider the trial-and-error approach
* Develop a decision matrix for evaluation
  * Timeliness
  * Risk
  * Manageability
  * Expense
  * Practicality
  * Effectiveness
* Ask your peers for help
* Step away from the problem
