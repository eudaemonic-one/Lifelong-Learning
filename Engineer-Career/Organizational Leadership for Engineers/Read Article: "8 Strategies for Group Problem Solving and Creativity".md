# Read Article: "8 Strategies for Group Problem Solving and Creativity"

Ref: https://engageforsuccess.org/effective-communication/strategies-for-group-problem-solving-and-creativity/

Startegies for better group problem solving:
* Make someone in charge.
* Select the right team.
* Mandate participation
* Assigne homework.
* Give people individual time to brainstorm.
* Keeping the meeting short.
* Set an agenda.
* Listen to all ideas.
