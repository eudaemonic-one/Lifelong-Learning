# Read Article: "How to Manage Stakeholder Conflict"

Ref: https://projectriskcoach.com/manage-stakeholder-conflict/

Project managers encounter conflict regularly over schedules, project priorities, human resources, requirements, and technical opinions.

5 Ways to Respond to Stakeholder Conflict:
* Withdrawing => conflict may surface later in the project
* Smoothing => emphasize the areas of agreement and fail to address differences of opinions
* Compromising => search for solutions where the stakeholders will compromise
* Forcing => lay down the law
* Problem-Solving => examine alternatives carefully.

Turn Conflict into a Problem-Solving Exercise:
* Seek to understand the differences of opinions and makes them transparent, carefully leading individuals and groups to find common ground.
* Problem-solving leads to a mutual commitment by the stakeholders with greater buy-in and support.
